I'm a junior year Electrical Engineering student at the Institute of Space Technology, Islamabad. My work sits at the intersection of embedded hardware and intelligent software — designing systems that make real decisions in real time on constrained devices.

My FYP is a vision-assisted Pointing and Tracking (PAT) system for indoor laser communication links — using a camera to re-find and re-point a laser transmitter whenever the link is disturbed or the receiver moves, with a gimbal keeping the beam locked on target. FYP · IN PROGRESS

Vision-Assisted Pointing & Tracking for Laser Comms

Final Year Project: an indoor Pointing and Tracking (PAT) system that keeps a free-space laser communication link alive. Phase 1 uses a camera to re-locate a stationary receiver and re-point the transmitter whenever the link is disturbed, resuming communication from where it dropped. Phase 2 extends this to a moving receiver, with a gimbal on the transmitter tracking and locking the beam onto the target in real time.
