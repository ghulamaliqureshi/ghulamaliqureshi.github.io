Longer term, I'm targeting an MS in Germany (TUM, RWTH Aachen, TU Berlin) — focused on embedded AI and autonomous systems.

Hybrid VLC and Wi-Fi Communication System with XOR Encryption

Designed and implemented a dual-mode optical-RF communication system on ESP32, leveraging Visible Light Communication as a primary channel with Wi-Fi fallback. XOR-based encryption layer applied at the hardware abstraction level. Demonstrated stable data transfer under ambient light interference conditions.
